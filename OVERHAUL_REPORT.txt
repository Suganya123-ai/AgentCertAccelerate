ARIA Book — Visual Overhaul Report
====================================
Date:  2026-05-20
Build: main.pdf (126 pages, copied to main-v2-visual.pdf)
Run:   3 iterations (style overhaul → TikZ figures → chapter banner polish)

Summary
-------
The book moved from a plain B&W serif draft to an Infosys-corporate
two-column layout: navy/teal palette, Fira Sans throughout, navy chapter
banners with a teal eyebrow, full-bleed navy pillar pages, and lightly
tinted callouts with a single accent rule. All seven `\imgplaceholder`
boxes were removed — five were replaced with original TikZ diagrams, one
with a small comparison table, and one (the benchmark/eval comparison)
was converted to a tabular figure. The bibliography stays as a single
consolidated list at the end.

-------------
- tn-style.sty                                  (full rewrite from plain → Infosys palette)

- parts/20-hardening-05-why.tex                 (TikZ: four loci of attack surface)
- parts/20-hardening-07-identity.tex            (TikZ: Zero Trust stack)
- parts/40-evaluation-13-vs-benchmarking.tex    (table: benchmark vs eval; TikZ: eval taxonomy 2x2)

- parts/50-certification-19-process.tex         (TikZ: governance triangle)
- parts/50-certification-20-keeping-certified.tex (TikZ: certification lifecycle)

No chapter prose was rewritten. Caption wording was lightly trimmed to read
correctly for the new diagrams.

What looks good
---------------
- Cover (PDF page 1): navy hero, teal accent column, Fira Sans display title,
  bottom strap with the Infosys mark. Reads enterprise-grade.
- TOC (page 3): clean two-tier hierarchy, navy entries on chapters, light
  rule under header, page numbers right-aligned.
- Chapter openers (e.g. PDF pages 14, 19, 33): one decorative element — a
  navy banner with white title, teal "CHAPTER N" eyebrow, and a 3pt teal
  rule below. Consistent across all twenty mainmatter chapters.
- Pillar dividers (PDF pages 13, 28, 45, 61, 87, 103): full navy page,
  large Fira Sans display title, teal accent at top, "ARIA FRAMEWORK · PILLAR"
  eyebrow.
- Callouts (Abstract, Definition, In Practice, Warning, Key takeaways,
  Practitioner checklist): tinted background + 3pt left rule. No
  blue-on-blue text. Warning uses amber; the rest use navy or teal.
- TikZ figures: clean, brand-aligned (navy fills + teal arrows), no shading
  gradients. See pages ~33 (attack surface), ~42 (Zero Trust stack), ~64
  (eval taxonomy), ~88 (cert stack), ~96 (governance triangle), ~98 (cert
  lifecycle).
- Header/footer: Infosys mark right, book title left, navy bold page number
  far right of footer, copyright far left. Thin teal rule under the header.

What's still imperfect
----------------------
1. Chapter end-pages occasionally have a key-takeaway/checklist block alone
   near the top with empty space below (e.g. PDF page 20 after the foreword).
   This is the float pattern: `\stoptwocol` + `figure*` + `\starttwocol`
   forces a column break and the rest of the chapter's body floats. It's
   tolerable but a future pass could tighten this by collapsing the float
   into the multicols (using `\captionof` inside the columns) for shorter
   figures.

2. 417 overfull `\hbox` warnings remain (down from ~540 in the plain build).
   None are fatal; most are 1–10pt overruns in two-column body. The
   `microtype` package is already loaded.

3. The named purple-toned illustrations in `ref_figures/extracted/Assets/`
   were intentionally NOT placed. They are stylized purple-on-dark art that
   would clash with the Infosys navy/teal palette. The five authored TikZ
   diagrams plus the existing benchmarking infographics cover the high-value
   visual slots; placing the named illustrations would have introduced
   off-brand color noise.

4. The 5 benchmarking PNGs in `figures/benchmarking/` were not re-styled.
   They were authored for the aria-2 book and have their own (compatible)
   navy/teal palette, so they pass; if a future pass wants to polish, they
   could be re-rendered to match the exact hex values.

5. Pillar divider title alignment is left-edge for visual weight; if the
   user prefers center alignment the change is one line in tn-style.sty
   (`anchor=west` → `anchor=center`).
